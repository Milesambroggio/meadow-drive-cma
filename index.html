How to position a 1969 home against newer comps: the expensive, un-fun items — roof, deck, garage, entry — are done, which is exactly what inspectors flag on homes this age. The interior is refreshed (paint, carpet, appliances) but not remodeled to H26's standard, and that gap is already reflected in the pricing above. Two things complete the story: (1) disclose the known foundation issue up front with an engineer's report and repair bids, so buyers price certainty instead of fear; (2) market the ~1,350 SF expansion allowance as the buyer's chance to create a custom, like-new home in a location where H26 just proved a remodeled product commands $1,197/SF.
