The Opportunity

At $1,499,000, this residence prices at the active-market average per square foot while undercutting both $1,695,000 listings — and it stays under the $1.5M search threshold where buyer traffic is deepest. The market has already proven the ceiling: a Meadow Drive townhome closed at $1,298/SF in 9 days at full price. What this unit asks of its price, it pays back in three ways no competitor matches at once: rental income flexibility, expansion upside, and a real garage.

Why It Commands a Premium

  • Short-term rentals permitted — investor & hybrid-use buyers can underwrite income
  • Expandable by ~1,350 SF — nearly double the home; add a primary suite & bath
  • Oversized garage + huge storage — rare below $1.5M in this comp set
  • New roof, rebuilt deck, new entry door, paint, carpet & kitchen appliances
  • High vaulted ceilings, gas fireplace, large private stone patio, western sun
  • Adjacent open space & Bighorn Park; walk to Racquet Club amenities & TOV bus

Priced-In Considerations

  • Interior is updated but not remodeled to the level of top comps
  • All 3 bedrooms upstairs share one full bath; second bath sits near the entry
  • Known foundation issues — disclose up front with an engineer's report and repair bids so buyers can price certainty, not fear
  • 1969 construction (roof & major exterior items recently addressed)
  • Expansion concept solves the bath split & finish gap — market it as the plan
